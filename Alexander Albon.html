Name:Alexander Albon Ansusinha

Birthdate:23 March 1996

Background:Thai-British racer, part of the Red Bull program. Made F1 debut in 2019 with Toro Rosso, promoted mid-season to Red Bull. Returned to the grid with Williams in 2022 after a year as Red Bull reserve. Known for smooth driving, strong racecraft, and leading Williams’ resurgence.
