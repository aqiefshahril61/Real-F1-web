Name:Oscar Jack Piastri

Birthdate:6 April 2001

Background:Junior career standout: won Formula Renault Eurocup (2019), Formula 3 (2020), and Formula 2 (2021) back-to-back. Made his F1 debut with McLaren in 2023. Scored his first podium and sprint win in his rookie season. Calm, fast, and seen as one of the brightest young stars on the grid.
