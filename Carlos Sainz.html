Name:Carlos Sainz Vázquez de Castro

Birthdate:1 September 1994

Background:Son of rally legend Carlos Sainz Sr. F1 debut in 2015 with Toro Rosso, later raced for Renault and McLaren. Joined Ferrari in 2021, won first F1 race at Silverstone 2022. Smart, consistent, and praised for race strategy and adaptability.
