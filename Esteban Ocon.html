Name:Esteban José Jean-Pierre Ocon-Khelfane

Birthdate:17 September 1996

Background:Made F1 debut in 2016 with Manor, later raced for Force India/Racing Point. Joined Renault/Alpine in 2020. Scored his first F1 victory at the 2021 Hungarian Grand Prix. Resilient, hardworking, and known for his defensive racecraft.
