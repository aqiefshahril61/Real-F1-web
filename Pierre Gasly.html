Name:Pierre Jean-Jacques Gasly

Birthdate:7 February 1996

Background:Made F1 debut in 2017 with Toro Rosso (now AlphaTauri). Briefly promoted to Red Bull in 2019 before returning to Toro Rosso. Took an emotional first F1 win at Monza 2020 with AlphaTauri. Talented, resilient, and admired for his comeback story.
